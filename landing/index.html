DATABASE LIFECYCLE

Choose its lifespan.

Burstable

Scratch space for an agent run or a one-off import. It expires after 24 hours of inactivity by default.

Persistent

Keep a database until you delete it. Snapshots run every six hours and are kept for seven days.
